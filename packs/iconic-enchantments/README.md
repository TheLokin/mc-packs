# Iconic Enchantments 2D

Enchanted books with visible enchantments and levels, preserving the vanilla aesthetic. Retains their original 2D appearance in all situations, including inventories, when held in hand and when displayed in the world, unlike the 3D models featured in [Iconic Enchantments 3D](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/iconic-enchantments-3d). Supports all Minecraft languages through enchantment icons added to the font and requires no CIT or OptiFine on Minecraft 1.21.5+.

## Enchanted Books & Tooltips

Books containing multiple enchantments display a cover based on the following priority:

1. Curses.
2. Enchantments with impossible levels.
3. Enchantments with the highest levels.

When multiple enchantments share the same priority, their names are sorted alphabetically. Enchantment icons appear in tooltips for easier identification and are supported in all Minecraft languages. Enchanted books also use different colors to indicate enchantment levels:

- **Blue:** Level 1.
- **Green:** Level 2.
- **Yellow:** Level 3.
- **Orange:** Level 4.
- **Red:** When the enchantment reaches its maximum obtainable level in Survival mode.
- **Purple:** When the enchantment exceeds its maximum obtainable level in Survival mode.

## Available Variants

Choose from three variants depending on your preferences:

- **Default (Books & Tooltips):** Includes both the modified enchanted book textures and enchantment icons in tooltips.
- **Books Only:** Includes the modified enchanted book textures without enchantment icons in tooltips.
- **Tooltips Only:** Includes enchantment icons in tooltips without modifying enchanted book textures.
