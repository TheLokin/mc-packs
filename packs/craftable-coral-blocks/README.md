# Craftable Coral Blocks

A simple and lightweight quality of life addition that allows you to craft all types of Coral Blocks, including their dead variants, making them easier to obtain without having to search for them underwater.

## Crafting Recipes

This adds standard vanilla crafting recipes to the crafting table:

- **4x Tube Coral or Tube Coral Fan** $\rightarrow$ **1x Tube Coral Block**
- **4x Brain Coral or Brain Coral Fan** $\rightarrow$ **1x Brain Coral Block**
- **4x Bubble Coral or Bubble Coral Fan** $\rightarrow$ **1x Bubble Coral Block**
- **4x Fire Coral or Fire Coral Fan** $\rightarrow$ **1x Fire Coral Block**
- **4x Horn Coral or Horn Coral Fan** $\rightarrow$ **1x Horn Coral Block**

The same recipes are available for all 5 Dead Coral variants.
