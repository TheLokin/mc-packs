# Craftable Coral Blocks

Adds vanilla crafting recipes for all Coral Block variants, including their dead counterparts. Each recipe allows you to craft a Coral Block using 4 pieces of its corresponding Coral or Coral Fan, providing a renewable way to obtain these blocks without having to destroy natural coral reefs.

## Crafting Recipes

- **4x Tube Coral or Tube Coral Fan** → **1x Tube Coral Block**
- **4x Brain Coral or Brain Coral Fan** → **1x Brain Coral Block**
- **4x Bubble Coral or Bubble Coral Fan** → **1x Bubble Coral Block**
- **4x Fire Coral or Fire Coral Fan** → **1x Fire Coral Block**
- **4x Horn Coral or Horn Coral Fan** → **1x Horn Coral Block**

The same recipes are available for all 5 Dead Coral variants.
