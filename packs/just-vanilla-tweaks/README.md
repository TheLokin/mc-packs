# Just Vanilla Tweaks

A bundle of vanilla-friendly tweaks that combines the features of several individual packs, covering recipes, drops, tool compatibility, dyeing and more.

## What does it change?

- **Stone variants:** Adds recipes for Calcite (Diorite + Dripstone Block), Tuff (Andesite + Diorite) and Cinnabar (Sulfur + Netherrack). Granite is now made from Cinnabar + Diorite and Diorite uses a shapeless Cobblestone + Quartz recipe.
- **Uncrafting:** Amethyst Blocks, Clay Blocks, Glowstone, Melons, Packed Ice, Blue Ice and Honeycomb Blocks can be converted back into 4 Amethyst Shards, 4 Clay Balls, 4 Glowstone Dust, 9 Melon Slices, 9 Ice, 9 Packed Ice and 4 Honeycomb respectively.
- **Snow:** Two Snow Blocks in a row can be crafted into 3 Snow, following the same pattern as Carpet.
- **Tool compatibility:** Glass, Stained Glass and Stained Glass Panes, Tinted Glass, Glowstone, Sea Lanterns, Beacons, Redstone Lamps, Frosted Ice and mob heads can now be mined faster with a pickaxe. Honeycomb Blocks and Froglights can now be mined faster with an axe.
- **Coral blocks:** Coral Blocks can be crafted from 4 of their corresponding Coral or Coral Fan, including all Dead Coral variants.
- **Copper oxidation:** Any Copper block or variant can be combined with a Sulfur Spike to advance it by one oxidation stage. Waxed variants are supported and keep their wax coating.
- **Stonecutter:** Adds Stonecutter recipes for blocks that have derived variants, such as Stairs, Slabs and Walls.
- **Dye rebalance:** Colored blocks and items can be re-dyed from any other color instead of only being craftable from their undyed or white variant. Whole blocks are re-dyed in batches of 8, while other items and variants are re-dyed individually.
- **Dye recipes:** Adds dye-based recipes for Red Sand, Red Sandstone, Smooth Red Sandstone, Red Nether Bricks, Nether Wart Blocks and Warped Wart Blocks, replacing the vanilla Red Nether Bricks and Nether Wart Block recipes. It also adds new ways to obtain Brown, Green, Lime and Black Dye, and makes Azure Bluet, Oxeye Daisy and White Tulip produce White Dye.
- **Cheaper recipes:** Shulker Boxes require 1 Shulker Shell and 1 Chest. Armor Trim duplication uses 1 primary material instead of 7 Diamonds. Pottery Sherds can be duplicated with 4 Bricks and the original Sherd to yield 2 copies.
