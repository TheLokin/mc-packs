# Dye Rebalance

Reworks dye recipes so colored blocks and items can be re-dyed rather than crafting them from their undyed or white variants. It also introduces dye-based crafting recipes for Red Sand, Red Sandstone, Red Nether Bricks, Nether Wart Blocks and Warped Wart Blocks, along with additional ways to obtain certain dyes.

## What does it change?

- **Re-dyeing:** Whole blocks such as Concrete, Concrete Powder, Glass, Terracotta and Wool can be recolored in batches of 8, while other items and block variants can be recolored individually. This keeps more refined variants relatively expensive by requiring more dye per item.
- **Dye-based recipes:** Adds recipes for Red Sand, Red Sandstone, Smooth Red Sandstone, Red Nether Bricks, Nether Wart Blocks and Warped Wart Blocks using their base materials and the corresponding dye. The existing Red Nether Bricks and Nether Wart Block recipes are replaced, while the Warped Wart Block recipe is entirely new.
- **Dyes:** Adds alternative ways to obtain Brown, Green, Lime and Black Dye by combining other dyes or using common materials. Additionally, Azure Bluet, Oxeye Daisy and White Tulip now produce White Dye instead of Light Gray Dye, better matching their appearance.

## Recipe consistency

All re-dyeing recipes follow a consistent set of rules. They are shapeless, allowing ingredients to be placed anywhere in the crafting grid. Recipes that use a single block or item must be placed before the dye, while recipes that recolor 8 items require the dye to be placed in the center slot.
