# Vanilla Cohesion

A bundle of vanilla-friendly tweaks that combines the features of several individual packs, covering recipes, drops, tool compatibility, dyeing and more.

## What does it change?

- **Stone variants:** Adds recipes for Calcite (Diorite + Dripstone Block), Tuff (Andesite + Diorite) and Cinnabar (Sulfur + Netherrack). Granite is now made from Cinnabar + Diorite and Diorite from Cobblestone + Quartz.
- **Stairs and slabs:** Quartz, Sandstone and Red Sandstone Stairs and Slabs are now crafted from their base blocks. This replaces the Chiseled variant recipes for these blocks, since those inputs did not match the resulting blocks.
- **Redstone recipes:** Dispensers, Droppers, Levers, Observers and Pistons can be crafted with Blackstone or Cobbled Deepslate in place of Cobblestone.
- **Snow:** Two Snow Blocks in a row craft 3 Snow, following the same pattern as Carpet.
- **Coral blocks:** Four matching Coral or Coral Fan craft one Coral Block and the same recipes are available for all Dead Coral variants.
- **Cheaper recipes:** Shulker Boxes require 1 Shulker Shell and 1 Chest and can be crafted in the inventory. Pottery Sherds can be duplicated with 4 Bricks and the original Sherd to yield 2 copies.
- **Armor trims:** Duplicating an Armor Trim template uses 7 blocks of the trim's primary material and 1 Diamond instead of 7 Diamonds and 1 primary material block.
- **Stonecutter:** Adds Stonecutter recipes for building blocks that have no vanilla stonecutting recipe, including stairs, slabs, walls, planks, logs and stripped variants.
- **Copper oxidation:** Any Copper block or variant can be combined with a Sulfur Spike to advance it by one oxidation stage. Waxed variants keep their wax coating.
- **Uncrafting:** Amethyst Blocks → 4 Amethyst Shards, Clay Blocks → 4 Clay Balls, Glowstone → 4 Glowstone Dust, Melons → 9 Melon Slices, Packed Ice → 9 Ice, Blue Ice → 9 Packed Ice and Honeycomb Blocks → 4 Honeycomb.
- **Clay drops:** Clay drops 2–4 Clay Balls and Fortune increases the amount.
- **Dye rebalance:** Colored blocks and items can be re-dyed from any other color. Full blocks such as Concrete, Concrete Powder, Glass, Terracotta and Wool are recolored 8 at a time, while other items and block variants are recolored individually.
- **Dye recipes:** Adds dye-based recipes for Red Sand and the full Red Sandstone family (including Chiseled, Cut and Smooth variants, Slabs, Stairs and Walls), Red Nether Bricks and their variants, Nether Wart Blocks and Warped Wart Blocks. The Red Nether Bricks and Nether Wart Block recipes are replaced, while the Warped Wart Block recipe is new. It also adds alternative ways to obtain Brown, Green, Lime and Black Dye and Azure Bluet, Oxeye Daisy and White Tulip now produce White Dye instead of Light Gray Dye.
- **Tool compatibility:** Glass, Stained Glass and Stained Glass Panes, Tinted Glass, Glowstone, Sea Lanterns, Beacons, Redstone Lamps, Frosted Ice and mob heads can be mined faster with a pickaxe. Honeycomb Blocks and Froglights with an axe.
- **Name tweaks:** Blocks such as Amethyst, Copper, Diamond and Raw Iron use the "X Block" naming and Eggs are renamed White Chicken Egg, Blue Chicken Egg and Brown Chicken Egg.
